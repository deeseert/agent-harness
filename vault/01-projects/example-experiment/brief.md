# Brief: sourdough starter kits (example)

> This is a made-up example to show the format. Delete it when you add your own project.

## The idea

A kit with a live sourdough starter, a jar, a scraper and a one-page guide, sold to people who just took a bread-making class.

## Who pays

People who finish a weekend bread-making class at two local cooking schools. They want to keep baking at home but don't have a starter.

## The test (14 days)

- Ask both schools if I can show the kit at the end of a class.
- Offer it at €25 with payment on the spot (card or instant transfer). Delivery the following week.
- Minimum code: a one-page site with a payment link, only if a school asks for one.

- **Continue:** at least 8 paid kits from people I don't know.

At most €150 for the first 10 kits.
